## Findings

### [MAJOR-1] WO-012 six-line ledgers clip their last line in both rendered frames
- File: src/main.gd:589 (fixed `custom_minimum_size.y = 86` with `fit_content = false`, `scroll_active = false`); new six-line texts at src/main.gd:1032-1039 and src/main.gd:1080-1088
- Problem: The `outcome_ledger` height was sized for the four-line WO-011 ledgers and was not adjusted for the new six-line WO-012 ledgers. In `captures/04-follow-up-decision.png` the `SIGNER  VENN.CREDENTIAL` row (src/main.gd:1038) is vertically truncated to its top half; in `captures/05-campaign-summary.png` the `DISPOSITION  STERILIZE` row (src/main.gd:1087) is likewise cut in half. This directly fails acceptance criterion 9 ("rendered follow-up/summary frames fit the 1280×720 target without clipping") and the request's own "How to verify" step 4 expected result ("the summary ledger has no clipping"), and it degrades criterion 6's payoff — the impossible `MINA.SOLANO` signer line on the purge route renders clipped too, since all three routes share this control.
- Evidence: Both PNGs regenerated by me at the implementation head and inspected; the sixth ledger row is half-height in each. Geometry: six rows at mono font size 12 need well over the 86 px minimum reserved at src/main.gd:589. The author's verification evidence claimed this check passed; it does not.
- Suggestion: Raise `custom_minimum_size.y` (or set it per-phase / enable `fit_content`) so six rows render fully, then regenerate and re-inspect both captures.

### [MINOR-1] Stale "UNTIL CASCADE" countdown persists through FOLLOWUP and SUMMARY
- File: src/main.gd:1124-1126 (clock refresh), src/main.gd:271-273 (static "UNTIL CASCADE" caption)
- Problem: `_refresh_all()` keeps rendering the WO-011 rupture countdown in the new phases — `04-follow-up-decision.png` shows "UNTIL CASCADE 03:00" and `05-campaign-summary.png` shows "UNTIL CASCADE 08:00" while the phase label reads "WO-012 DISPOSITION" / "ENDURANCE RECALCULATED". The cascade window is resolved once a repair outcome locks; a player can read the header as a live timer pressuring the WO-012 choice, which contradicts the days-based endurance framing of the new decision.
- Evidence: Both regenerated captures show the leftover mm:00 countdown in the header alongside WO-012 phase labels.
- Suggestion: Freeze, blank, or relabel the clock (e.g. "WINDOW CLOSED") once `phase >= Phase.FOLLOWUP`.

### [NIT-1] Inconsistent label for the same unsupported-Jae state
- File: src/main.gd:1281-1284 vs src/main.gd:962-966
- `_campaign_jae_state()` reports `CRITICAL / MED-2` for the unsupported, pre-T+10 case while the WO-011 isolate ledger calls the same state `CRITICAL / SIGNAL PRESENT`. Harmless, but a shared constant or one label would keep the M1 carryover visibly traceable to the WO-011 ledger.

## What I did NOT check

- Interactive play in a live window: mouse clicks, focus traversal, and the `N`/`S`/`Q`/`R` keys as real engine-loop input events. verify.gd calls `open_next_order()`/`resolve_followup()` directly for most routes and synthesizes only one `InputEventKey` (S on the purge route); the OUTCOME-phase `N` key handler was never driven as an event.
- Cross combinations of route × disposition: verify.gd covers sterilize-on-purge and quarantine-on-bypass; sterilize/quarantine on the isolation route (and quarantine-on-purge) are untested, though `resolve_followup` is route-independent.
- Low-coherence interference on the bypass and isolation routes (only the purge route's interference text is asserted and rendered); likewise the low-coherence `VOICE MATCH / MINA.SOLANO` reveal appearing on a sub-50-coherence non-purge route is a reachable presentation state I did not enumerate.
- Rendering at window sizes other than the 1280×720 target, or under renderers other than GL Compatibility on this WSL2/D3D12 Mesa stack; captures 01–03 were only checked for successful generation, not inspected pixel by pixel.
- Godot versions other than 4.4.1.stable.mono.official.
- Long-session behavior across many `restart_shift()` scene reloads (leaks, accumulated state).
